The system

Built on Hyperliquid L1 perpetuals. Sub-second settlement, deep orderbooks, no custody risk. The engine runs 24/7, scanning seven liquid instruments across three horizons simultaneously.

Each timeframe carries a different job. The 5m layer catches fast expansion. The 15m layer is the workhorse, balancing signal frequency with signal quality. The 1h layer trades only high-conviction structure. Position size scales with timeframe: smaller on noise, larger on conviction.

How the edge is built

Most traders lose because they size by conviction. I size by mathematics. Trading principle

Stops are volatility-adaptive. A fixed percentage stop treats a 0.3% move the same on every asset, which is wrong. Every stop sits at ATR(14) multiplied by a per-instrument factor, so risk stays constant across instruments with wildly different volatility profiles.

Targets are R-multiples, not price guesses. Each setup defines its own reward ratio, validated against measured hit rate. A 1:4 target at 31% win rate beats a 1:1 target at 60%. The math is not close.

Exits stay untouched. I tested breakeven locks, partial take-profits, and trailing stops across 618 trades. All three destroyed performance. A trend-following book with 1:4 targets survives on a handful of large winners. Cutting them short removes the entire edge.

Correlation is treated as position sizing. Instruments that move together are capped together. Five correlated positions is one position at five times the size.

R
Risk-First Sizing
Fixed-fractional risk per trade, weighted by measured edge and sample confidence. Position size is an output, never an input.
σ
Volatility-Normalized Stops
ATR-scaled stops keep risk constant across instruments. Liquidation buffer verified at multiple times the widest stop.
ρ
Correlation Clustering
Instruments grouped by measured PnL correlation. Exposure capped per cluster, not per position.
